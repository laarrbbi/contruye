## En la promoción

After the works are finished and received (Módulo 6 ends with «obra terminada, certificado final de obra visado y acta de recepción firmada») and before the sale deeds (Módulo 11). The course gives no month for it: it puts the final inspection «antes de pedir nada» and recalls that the obra nueva en construcción was declared «al empezar». The «Dónde ir» plazos run from «1-2 semanas» (visado del CFO) and «1-4 semanas» (Industria, registro de certificados energéticos) to «1-3 meses» (primera ocupación; «la declaración responsable es inmediata en eficacia, con comprobación posterior»), «1-2 meses» (cédula), «1-3 semanas» (notaría), «15 días hábiles (prorrogables)» (Registro de la Propiedad) and «1-6 meses» (Catastro). Whole promotion: «Duración típica: 3-4 años desde la compra del suelo hasta la última escritura» (Módulo 2).

## El solar

**Al empezar:** As Module 8 ends (course_world.php, module 8 end): the finished building of the course's example (planta baja + 5 stepped to the street: excavation, foundation, six floors of structure and façade, roof with panels and the lift housing, installations, finishes) on its new street with the portal «EDIFICIO CONSTRUYE» (our sign; the owner names the building); crane, huts and fence gone since Module 6.5; the CONSTRUYE sales office beside it (place.oficina_ventas) and the green dots of the homes sold (site.sold). In the promoter's folder: the CFO visado and the acta de recepción (Module 6), the quality documentation (Module 7), the private contracts and one file per buyer (Module 8). No home can yet be deeded on its own or lived in.

**Al terminar:** The same building, sales office and sold dots, now legal: the homes split into fincas (site.units during 9.2; the current engine ends Module 9 without site.units, so they can fade to a thin outline), the primera ocupación posted at the portal (and, where the community requires it, a cédula on each home), the energy label at the sales office, the decenal shield solid over the structure, the bank's mortgage split per finca, the Catastro's map drawing the building, and on the promoter's desk the Libro del Edificio and the expediente de garantías. Resultado: «primera ocupación (y cédula donde se exige), escritura de obra nueva terminada y división horizontal inscritas, Libro del Edificio.»

## El guion, en una página

Start where Module 8 left the world: the finished EDIFICIO CONSTRUYE, the CONSTRUYE sales office beside it and the green dots of the homes sold; morning light. The module plays as a paper chase that turns a finished building into homes that can be deeded, mortgaged and lived in (the objective: «convertir la obra terminada en viviendas que se pueden escriturar, hipotecar y habitar»), with one big visual turn in the middle: the building splitting into its homes.
9.1 · Eight empty card frames (the lesson's table «Documento | Quién lo emite») hang over the roof; the camera hops Colegios → regional energy registry → OCT and each frame fills with a stamp. Back on site the façade goes to cutaway for the inspection «antes de pedir nada» (the Module 3 blueprint laid over what was built, gold defect marks that pop and go out), the networks light up as they are tested and their certificates come back registered from Industria, the lift runs and the supplies become definitive; the last cards drop into a navy CONSTRUYE binder.
9.2 · A sepia flashback to Module 5 for the obra nueva en construcción; then the notary's folder of what the obra nueva terminada needs; the building splits into its coloured homes (site.units), each with its card and cuota; the estatutos; one deed signed; the Registro tags each home as a finca, the bank's mortgage threads split per finca and a paper flies to the Catastro; coins for arancel and AJD under the ribbon «No se puede repercutir al comprador consumidor»; the energy label goes to the sales office.
9.3 · Ayuntamiento: the Module 4 licence laid over the building, two doors (licencia / declaración responsable) with the student's community lit, the folder of documents (hop to the Catastro; the definitive ICIO coin), the primera ocupación posted at the portal; the map of the 8 cédula communities and, where it applies, a cédula landing on each home; three red warnings that turn green (the supply company's months).
9.4 · The binder grows into the LIBRO DEL EDIFICIO with the CONSTRUYE mark; its chapters fill; calendar badges on the lift, PCI, thermal and electrical systems; the book tagged for the community and an extract flying to each home.
9.5 · At the Aseguradora the decenal shield turns solid gold; warranty tags rise from aerotermia, lift, windows and waterproofing; the agents' chain with their contracts; the expediente de garantías splits per buyer and community; a «procedimiento de incidencias» card points to Module 12. Close on an orbit of the legal building and the Resultado stamp (as Module 1 closes on its own Resultado).
Throughout: the counters climb (papers; taxes: ICIO, AJD, arancel, seguro decenal; checklist 11/11) and the timeline shows each institution's «Dónde ir» plazo, from 1-2 semanas to 1-6 meses. Partners pop up in 9.2 (notario, abogado and the directory's abogados) and the Sede Electrónica del Catastro link in 9.3; the institutions carry the names of the student's province from Anexo A, Fase 9 (ayuntamiento, organismo de la cédula, Colegio Notarial, Registro de la Propiedad, registro de certificados energéticos, Servicio de Industria).
Order note for the script: the course reads 9.2 (the deed) before 9.3 and 9.4, yet its own «Necesitas» list says the deed needs the primera ocupación and, where the regional law asks for it, the Libro del Edificio, and the definitive decenal comes in 9.5. Either keep those cards dashed in 9.2 and fill them as 9.3–9.5 bring them while the signature plays as the lesson describes it, or hold the notary's and registrar's stamps for a closing step after 9.5, so the student never sees the deed signed with a paper missing. Some places are hops inside a step rather than steps of their own, because no lesson sentence of their own names them: the supply companies in 9.1, the bank and the Catastro in 9.2, the Catastro again in 9.3; if the engine keeps one place per step, split them into short steps that reuse the step's quote.

## Impuestos, tasas y garantías

- **AJD** — With the escritura de declaración de obra nueva terminada y división horizontal (Lección 9.2), on the value of the obra nueva and of the división horizontal; the rate of the student's community (Anexo B; legal parameter of the region). Not chargeable to the consumer buyer.

  > **Coste:** notaría y registro según arancel, AJD (Anexo B) sobre el valor de la obra nueva y de la división horizontal. **No se puede repercutir al comprador consumidor.**

- **notaría y registro según arancel** — The notary's fees for the deed and the registry's for its inscription (Lección 9.2). Not chargeable to the consumer buyer.

  > **Coste:** notaría y registro según arancel, AJD (Anexo B) sobre el valor de la obra nueva y de la división horizontal. **No se puede repercutir al comprador consumidor.**

- **ICIO** — Its liquidación definitiva goes with the application for the primera ocupación (Lección 9.3); Module 4 paid the provisional one.

  > Documentos habituales: CFO, certificados de instalaciones, certificado energético, fotos, alta en el Catastro o justificante, certificado de las acometidas, liquidación definitiva del ICIO.

- **seguro decenal** — The policy becomes definitive after the OCT's final report (9.1 → 9.5); the deed of obra nueva terminada needs it («Seguro decenal (art. 20 LOE).», Lección 9.2).

  > **Completa el seguro decenal:** póliza definitiva tras el informe final del OCT.

### Lección 9.3 — Primera ocupación y cédula de habitabilidad

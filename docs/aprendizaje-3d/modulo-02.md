## El solar

**Al empezar:** Exactly as Module 1 ended: the SPE's plot (optioned and inscribed) with the matched green outline and the SPE flag, the PGOU envelope with the white volumetry inside, the utility connection points, the servitude and flood checks, any charge tags, the «precio máximo del suelo» tag from the CRP preliminar; the Module 1 dossier in the office; no works.

**Al terminar:** Still no works, but the project is decided and the land is the SPE's. Result: «Cuenta de Resultado Promotor (CRP) con tres escenarios y decisión GO / NO GO.»; 12/12 ticks. Next, Module 3: «Proyecto básico y de ejecución visados».

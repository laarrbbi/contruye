# Handover of the keys

> **Objective:** sign, get paid, set up the owners' association and hand over homes that can be lived in from day one.
> **Duration:** 115 min · **Result:** deeds signed, development loan repaid, owners' association set up and handover records.

## Laws that apply

| Rule | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|
| Civil Code (*Código Civil*), arts. 1445 et seq., 1455 | Sale and the legal split of the deed costs |
| TRLGDCU, art. 89.3 | Costs and taxes you cannot pass on |
| Ley 5/2019, art. 14 | Mortgage costs: the bank pays the notary, registry and agency costs of the mortgage; the buyer pays the valuation and their copies |
| RDL 1/1993 (ITP-AJD) + RD-ley 17/2018 | The bank pays the AJD on the mortgage. The buyer pays the AJD on the purchase of a new home |
| LPH (Ley 49/1960) | Owners' association, general meeting, reserve fund (at least 10 % of the ordinary budget, art. 9.1.f, since Ley 8/2013) |
| TRLRHL, arts. 104-110 | Municipal plusvalía: the developer pays it as the seller |
| Ley 10/2010 and Ley 7/2012 | Identification of payments and the cash limit |

## Where to go

| Institution | What you do |
|---|---|
| **Notary** | Signing of the sale and, where relevant, the buyer's mortgage or a partial cancellation |
| **Land Registry** | Registration in the buyer's name (handled by the notary or the agency) |
| **AEAT** | NIF of the owners' association (form 036) |
| **Bank** | The owners' association's account |
| **Distributor and retailers** | Electricity contracts for each home (with the CIE and first occupation or cédula) |
| **Water company** | Water contracts for each home |
| **Town hall** | Self-assessment of the plusvalía (30 working days from each sale) and change of IBI taxpayer |
| **Association of Property Managers** | A property manager for the owners' association |

## Lesson 11.1 — The deed of sale

- **Contents:** parties, registered property and description, price and payment terms (with earlier payments identified), encumbrances (free of encumbrances, or with the take-over chosen by the buyer), statements (ten-year insurance, licences, energy certificate, cédula), costs.
- **Who pays what:**

| Cost | Buyer | Developer |
|---|---|---|
| IVA 10 % (IGIC in Canarias, IPSI in Ceuta and Melilla) | ✔ (you charge it) | — |
| AJD on the sale (Annex B) | ✔ | — |
| Notary for the sale | Their copy | The original deed (art. 1455 CC, unless agreed otherwise; with a consumer, do not impose costs that are yours) |
| Registration of their purchase | ✔ | — |
| The buyer's mortgage | Valuation and copies | The bank pays AJD, notary, registry and agency |
| Municipal plusvalía | — | ✔ (cannot be passed on) |
| Cancellation of the developer's mortgage | — | ✔ |
| New building and horizontal division | — | ✔ |
| General utility connections | — | ✔ |

- **Payment:** named banker's draft or a transfer confirmed at the notary's. If the buyer takes over the loan, their share becomes their mortgage and the bank deducts that amount.
- **Development loan:** with each deed, that property's share is cancelled or taken over. When the last home is sold, it must be at zero.

## Lesson 11.2 — The owners' association

- **It comes into being** with the first sale: from then on there is more than one owner. While you still have unsold homes, you pay their service charges.
- **Steps:**
  1. Call the founding meeting (with an agenda).
  2. Election of the chair and, if decided, a secretary and a property manager.
  3. Approval of the ordinary budget and the charges by share.
  4. **Reserve fund** of at least 10 % of the ordinary budget.
  5. The owners' association's NIF from the AEAT, and a bank account.
  6. Minute book validated at the Land Registry.
  7. Maintenance contracts: lift, PCI, cleaning, landscaping, building insurance.
- **Property manager:** advisable from day one. Ask for a registered one.

## Lesson 11.3 — Utilities and final payment

| Utility | What you need | Who signs up |
|---|---|---|
| Electricity | Registered CIE, CUPS for each home, first occupation or cédula | Each buyer, with their retailer (the developer can leave it ready) |
| Water | First occupation or cédula, individual meter | The buyer, with the municipal company |
| Gas (if any) | Installation certificate | The buyer |
| Telecoms | Certified ICT | The buyer, with their operator |

- **Watch out:** electricity contracts can be held up for weeks if the permanent connection or the transformer station are not legalised. Start with the distributor in Module 6.

## Lesson 11.4 — Handing over possession

- **On the day of signing or by appointment:**
  - Final visit with the buyer and check of the snags.
  - **Record of handover of possession** signed (date, condition, meter readings, keys and remotes handed over, documents).
  - Walk-through of the home: consumer unit, aerothermal heat pump, stopcocks, maintenance.
- **Meter readings** in the record, and a note of outstanding incidents with the commitment to resolve them.
- **Warranties:** the LOE's 1, 3 and 10-year periods run **from handover of the works without reservations, or from when the reservations are remedied** (art. 17.1 LOE), not from handover to each buyer. Explain it to them in writing.

## Lesson 11.5 — Formalities after signing

- **Taxes on the sale:** the buyer pays the AJD on their community's form 600 within the regional deadline (usually 30 working days); many notaries handle it. You declare the IVA charged on your form 303.
- **Registry:** the notary sends an electronic copy to the Land Registry the same day; then the copy with the tax paid is filed and registered in the buyer's name.
- **Cadastre:** the notary and the registry report the change of owner; no declaration needs filing.
- **Municipal plusvalía:** self-assess it within 30 working days for each sale (Module 12).
- **Utilities:** new contracts or changes of holder into the buyer's name.
- **Documents:** hand over what belongs to the buyer and to the owners' association's property manager.

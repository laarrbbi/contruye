# Sales and marketing

> **Objective:** sell off-plan the homes the bank requires, and the rest before handover, complying with consumer law, anti-money-laundering rules and the guarantees.
> **Duration:** 110 min · **Result:** sales plan, legal sales documentation, private contracts with guarantees and CRM.

## Laws that apply

| Rule | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|
| **RD 515/1989**, consumer information on the sale of homes | What you must have available for the buyer: drawings, specification, total price, payment terms, registry details, licence |
| **TRLGDCU (RDL 1/2007)**, art. 61 | Advertising is binding: what you advertise is part of the contract |
| **TRLGDCU, art. 89.3** | Unfair terms: **you cannot charge the buyer** the municipal plusvalía, the costs of the new building and horizontal division, the developer's mortgage and its cancellation, or the connections to the general utilities; **nor force them to take over** your loan |
| **LOE, DA 1.ª** | Individual guarantee and special account for everything collected before handover |
| **Ley 12/2023**, art. 31 | Minimum information for the buyer in a home purchase |
| **RD 390/2021** | The energy rating must appear in the advertising |
| **Ley 10/2010** (money laundering) | The developer is an obliged entity: identify the buyer and the beneficial owner, verify the origin of the funds, keep records for 10 years |
| **Ley 7/2012** (amended by Ley 11/2021) | Cash payments banned from 1.000 € when one party is a business |
| Regional laws | Compulsory register of estate agents in Cataluña, the Comunitat Valenciana and Canarias (Annex A); regional consumer rules on housing |
| **Ley 5/2019** | The buyer's mortgage: information, deadlines and costs |

## Lesson 8.1 — Marketing and launch

| Development milestone | Sales action |
|---|---|
| Land purchase | Brand, holding website, lead capture |
| Basic design | Soft launch: list of interested buyers, no money taken |
| Licence applied for | Official launch: website, portals, agencies, sales office |
| Licence granted + guarantees ready | **Private contracts with guaranteed advance payments** |
| Start of works | "Works under way" announcement |
| Structure finished | Site visits with hard hats, second wave of sales |
| Last units | Adjust price or product |

- **Materials:** renders, dimensioned plans for each unit type, specification, PDF brochure, video, virtual tour, show flat (physical or virtual).
- **Channels:** your own website with a form, Idealista, Fotocasa, pisos.com, Google Ads, Meta Ads, your own database, foreign buyers (international portals) on the coast.
- **Compulsory in every advert:** the design's energy rating, and nothing you cannot deliver (advertising is binding).
- **Have the RD 515/1989 documentation ready** in the sales office before you advertise.

- **Pricing policy:** set the opening prices and a review policy by sales phase (for example, raise them 2–3 % on reaching 30 % and 60 % sold), and stick to it.
- **Organise the sale:** sales office, viewings, phone enquiries and customer management.

## Lesson 8.2 — Sales channel

- **In-house team:** sales director, sales staff, customer service. More control and margin; a fixed cost.
- **Collaborating agencies:**
  - Usual commission: 2,5–3 % on standard homes, 3–5 % at the top end.
  - Written collaboration agreement: commission, when it is earned (at the deed or in stages), exclusivity, minimum prices, use of the brand.
  - In Cataluña, the Comunitat Valenciana and Canarias, check that the agency is on the **compulsory regional register**.
- **Mixed:** your own office + a network of agencies. The most common.

## Lesson 8.3 — Reservation and private contract

- **Reservation:**
  - Amount: 3.000–10.000 €.
  - It is already an advance payment: **it must be guaranteed** (DA 1.ª LOE).
  - Deadline to sign the private contract: 7–30 days.
  - Set out what happens to the money if the contract is not signed.
- **Private sale contract · practical contents:**
  1. Parties identified (DNI/NIE; beneficial owner if a company).
  2. Home: number, floor, usable and built areas, appurtenances (garage, storeroom), plan and specification as appendices.
  3. Total price with **IVA 10 % shown separately** (IGIC in Canarias, IPSI in Ceuta and Melilla).
  4. Payment calendar.
  5. **Details of the guarantee** (individual bank guarantee or surety insurance) and of the **special account**.
  6. Building licence (number and date).
  7. Expected handover date and the consequences of delay.
  8. The buyer's right to terminate and get back what they paid, plus interest, if the home is not delivered on time.
  9. Costs: the buyer pays their own (copy of the deed, registration of their purchase, their mortgage). **Do not** charge them the costs that art. 89.3 TRLGDCU forbids.
  10. Taking over the development loan: only as an option, never compulsory.
  11. Choice of notary: the consumer buyer's right.
  12. Options and extras in an appendix, with price and deadline.
- **Typical payment plan:**

| Moment | % of price |
|---|---|
| Reservation | 1–3 % |
| Private contract | 5–10 % |
| Payments during the works | 10–20 % |
| Deed | 70–85 % |

- **Anti-money-laundering checks (Ley 10/2010) on every sale:**
  - Copy of the DNI or NIE.
  - Beneficial owner if a company is buying.
  - Declaration of the origin of the funds, with evidence if the amount is significant.
  - Every payment through a bank, never in cash above the legal limit.
  - Keep the documents for 10 years.
- ⚠️ **Typical mistakes:** collecting the reservation without a guarantee; charging the plusvalía to the buyer (the clause is void); promising a specification in the advertising that you later change.

- **Changes the buyers ask for:** accept them only if they are technically feasible, the site supervision team authorises them and they go into an appendix with price and deadline.
- **Record** every payment, guarantee, incident and contract change for each buyer.
- **Coordinate** the documents with the lawyers, the bank and the insurer of the advance payments.

## Lesson 8.4 — CRM and extras

- **CRM:** each prospect with source, unit type wanted, budget, financing, status and next action.
- **Keeping in touch with buyers during the works:**
  - Welcome, with a named contact.
  - Monthly photo or video of progress.
  - An update at each milestone (structure, roof, finishes).
  - 3-4 months before finishes: choice of options.
  - 1 month before the deed: date and documents.
- **Options and extras:** a closed catalogue (2-3 floor finishes, upgraded kitchen, wardrobes, home automation) with price and deadline. No changes accepted after the deadline.

- **Sales follow-up:**
  - Track homes reserved, sold and available every week.
  - Keep buyers informed of construction milestones and the expected handover date.
  - Coordinate **each buyer's mortgage** in good time (valuation, offer, signing).
  - **Cancellations:** apply the contract (refund through the guarantee if the breach is yours; the agreed penalty if it is the buyer's).
  - **Substitutions (assignment of contract):** only with your consent, in writing, and with a new guarantee in the assignee's name.
  - Open an **individual file for each buyer** from the reservation: it is the basis of the handover file.

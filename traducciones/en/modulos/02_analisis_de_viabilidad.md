# Feasibility study

> **Objective:** decide on the numbers whether the deal goes ahead, and at what land price.
> **Duration:** 130 min · **Result:** developer's profit and loss account (CRP) with three scenarios and a GO / NO GO decision.

## Laws that apply

| Rule | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|
| Ley 37/1992 (IVA), art. 91 | IVA 10 % on the sale of homes and on construction work on homes; 4 % on protected housing under the special scheme |
| Ley 27/2014 (IS) + Ley 7/2024 | IS rates (see lesson 2.4) |
| RDL 2/2004 (TRLRHL), arts. 100-103 | ICIO up to 4 % of the actual cost of the works |
| Orden ECO/805/2003 | Mortgage valuation. The bank's valuers use it, residual method included |
| RD 1492/2011 | Valuation regulation of the Land Act (expropriations and planning valuations) |

## Where to go

| Institution | What you ask for | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Approved valuation company** (Banco de España register) | Valuation of the land and of the finished building ("finished building hypothesis") | The value the bank will use |
| **Bank** (Annex A, Phase 2) | A preliminary conversation about financing | Indicative terms and pre-sales requirements |
| **Registry Statistics** (*Estadística Registral*, registradores.org) and the **Notaries' Statistics Portal** | Prices recorded in deeds by area | Real prices, not asking prices |
| **Cadastre** | Reference value of properties in the area | The reference the tax office uses for ITP |
| **Town hall** | Tax by-law for the ICIO and fees | The exact ICIO rate in the municipality |

## Lesson 2.1 — The developer's profit and loss account (CRP)

- **Structure:**
```
  REVENUE (sale of homes, garages, storerooms, commercial units; without IVA)
− Land (price + non-recoverable taxes + notary + registry + due diligence)
− Construction (PEC without IVA)
− External works and utility connections
− Professional fees (design, site supervision, CSS, engineering, OCT)
− Licences, ICIO and fees
− Insurance (ten-year, TRC, RC, surety)
− Sales and marketing (agencies, marketing, show flat)
− Overheads and management costs
− Finance costs
= PROFIT BEFORE TAX
− Corporate income tax (Impuesto sobre Sociedades)
= NET PROFIT
```
- **Work without IVA** in the CRP: the IVA you pay is recovered when you charge 10 % on the sales.
- **Indicative weights as a share of revenue:**

| Item | % of revenue |
|---|---|
| Land | 15–30 % |
| Construction | 45–55 % |
| Professional fees | 5–8 % |
| Licences and fees | 2–4 % |
| Finance costs | 3–6 % |
| Sales and marketing | 2–4 % |
| Overheads | 3–5 % |
| Insurance | 1–2 % |
| **Profit before tax** | **15–25 %** |

- **Rule:** below a 15 % margin on sales, the risk is not worth it.

## Lesson 2.2 — Fit and revenue: the market study

- **Fit study first ("mono").** Ask the architect for a capacity study or *test fit*: in the trade it is called "ordering a *mono*".
  - It estimates the buildability that counts and the built and usable areas.
  - It lays out the homes, common areas, stair and lift cores, garages and storerooms.
  - It checks unit types, daylight, ventilation and accessibility.
  - It tells you whether an additional planning or management instrument is needed.
  - Without a *mono*, the CRP is guesswork.

- **Areas:**
  - Usable: what the buyer walks on.
  - Built: usable + walls (≈ usable × 1,15–1,20).
  - Built including common areas: + a share of entrance halls, stairs and lifts (≈ usable × 1,25–1,35). This is the one usually advertised.
  - Computable: the one that counts towards buildability under your PGOU.
- **How to get the sale price:**
  1. Visit the developments on sale within 1-3 km. Call as a buyer. Note €/m², unit type, specification and sales pace.
  2. Compare with the prices recorded in deeds by the Registrars and Notaries (not asking prices).
  3. Read the quarterly reports of Tinsa, Sociedad de Tasación, Gloval and the consultancies (CBRE, JLL, Savills, Colliers).
  4. Apply a 3-5 % discount to the competition's asking prices for your first off-plan sales.
- **Indicative product mix:**

| Unit type | % | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 1 bedroom (50-65 m²) | 10–20 % | Highest €/m², investors |
| 2 bedrooms (70-85 m²) | 35–40 % | The most in demand |
| 3 bedrooms (90-110 m²) | 35–40 % | Families |
| 4 bedrooms (120 m²+) | 5–10 % | High ticket, slow turnover |
| Penthouses | depends on the building | 20-40 % premium |
| Garage | per space | 10.000–35.000 € depending on the city |
| Storeroom | per unit | 3.000–8.000 € |

- ⚠️ **Typical mistakes:** using advertised prices; designing the mix the developer likes rather than the one the area wants; forgetting that unsold garages stay on the balance sheet.

- **What you must also measure in the market:**
  - **Absorption:** homes sold per month per comparable development, and the total selling period.
  - **Future supply:** developments in the area with a licence granted or in process (ask at the town hall and look at the architects' association's statistics on endorsed designs).
  - **Buyer profile** and demand by unit type and size.
  - **Separate prices** for the home, the garage and the storeroom.
  - Always check advertised prices against real transactions.

## Lesson 2.3 — Costs, precisely

- **Land:** price + non-recoverable ITP or AJD + notary + registry + topographic survey + geotechnical study + lawyers + finance cost of the land + urbanisation charges.
- **Residual method:** `Maximum land price = Revenue − all other costs − target profit`. The land is worth what the project can afford to pay.
- **Construction (material execution cost, 2025-2026, above ground):**
  - Standard: 900–1.100 €/m² built
  - Upper-mid: 1.100–1.400 €/m²
  - Luxury: from 1.400 €/m²
  - Basement: 500–800 €/m² (more if there is groundwater or retaining walls)
  - Always ask local contractors for prices: they vary a lot by province.
- **Budgets:**
  - **PEM** (material execution budget): the architect calculates it in the design.
  - **PEC** (contract budget) = PEM + overheads (13–17 %) + contractor's profit (6 %). That benchmark comes from public procurement (RD 1098/2001); on private works it is negotiated.
  - IVA on the works: 10 % (construction of homes contracted by the developer, art. 91.Uno.3.1.º LIVA). It is recoverable.
- **Other costs:**

| Item | Benchmark |
|---|---|
| Architect (design + works direction) | 3–5 % of the PEM (fees are free) |
| Technical architect (direction of works execution) | 1,5–3 % of the PEM |
| Health and safety coordination | 0,5–1 % of the PEM |
| Engineering (structure, building services) | 1–2 % of the PEM |
| Geotechnical study | 2.000–8.000 € |
| OCT | 0,3–0,6 % of the PEM |
| ICIO | up to 4 % of the actual cost of the works (check the by-law) |
| Licence fee | per by-law |
| Ten-year insurance | 0,8–1,5 % of the construction cost |
| TRC + RC | 0,2–0,5 % |
| Guarantees or insurance for advance payments | 0,5–1,5 % a year on the amount guaranteed |
| Notary and registry for new building and horizontal division | per official scale |
| Sales and marketing | 2–4 % of sales |
| Finance costs | 3–6 % of revenue |

- ⚠️ **Typical mistakes:** leaving out utility connections (electricity and water can cost tens of thousands of euros); forgetting the cost of guarantees; putting the ICIO on the PEM without reading the by-law.

- **Contingencies:** set aside 3–5 % of the construction cost from the very first CRP.
- **Legal costs** (lawyers, notary, registry) and **corporate structure** costs: include them even if they look small.
- To put all this in methodically, use the model in the **FINANCIALS** section: it calculates the cost by chapter, the loan and the cash flow.

## Lesson 2.4 — Ratios and the decision

- **Margin on sales** = profit before tax ÷ revenue. Minimum 15 %.
- **ROE** = net profit ÷ equity. Target > 20 % a year.
- **Project and equity IRR (TIR):** on the monthly cash flow. Target 15–20 % for the project, 20 %+ on equity.
- **Typical duration:** 3-4 years from buying the land to the last deed.
- **Corporate income tax (IS, periods starting in 2026, Ley 7/2024):**
  - General: 25 %
  - Newly created entity: 15 % in the first period with a positive tax base and the next one
  - Small company (turnover < 10 M€): 23 %
  - Micro-company (< 1 M€): 19 % on the first 50.000 € and 21 % on the rest
  - Navarra and País Vasco: their own foral rules
  - Confirm it with your adviser: there are exclusions (asset-holding entities), and a development's turnover varies a lot from one year to the next.
- **Sensitivity scenarios:**

| Scenario | Assumptions |
|---|---|
| Optimistic | Sales at 100 % of price, construction on budget |
| Base | Sales at 95 %, construction +5 % |
| Pessimistic | Sales at 85–90 %, construction +10–15 %, 6 months' delay |

- **Decision:** GO only if the pessimistic case still makes no loss.
- ⚠️ **Typical mistakes:** calculating the margin with IVA included; leaving out the calendar (IRR depends on time); forgetting IS in the ROE.

## Lesson 2.5 — Negotiation, structure and approval of the investment

- **Negotiating with the plot's owner:**
  - Price, payment terms (cash, deferred with a guarantee, partly in homes), deadlines.
  - **Exclusivity** and enough time to do the studies and get financing: sign it in a letter of intent or reservation document.
  - **Conditions precedent:** planning, registry, technical and financial feasibility; obtaining the licence.
  - Formulas: direct sale, option to buy, **swap** for future homes, or **contribution of the land** to the company in exchange for shares.
- **Legal and corporate structure:**
  - **New SPV or existing company.** The SPV ring-fences the risk and makes it easier for partners to come in and go out; the existing company brings its banking track record.
  - **Partners and contributions:** capital or shareholder loan. A participating loan (RDL 7/1996, art. 20) counts as equity for company-law purposes.
  - **Shareholders' agreement:** reinforced majorities, division of roles, additional contributions if there are overruns, deadlock, exit (drag-along and tag-along), distribution of profits.
  - Notarial **powers of attorney** for whoever manages, and the **guarantees** the banks ask for (personal guarantees).
  - Review the **tax** treatment of the purchase and of the development before signing.
- **Approving the investment:**
  - Prepare an **investment memorandum**: plot, product, CRP, cash flow, sensitivities, risks, calendar and financing.
  - The investment committee or the **shareholders' meeting** approves it. In a capital company, buying an asset worth more than 25 % of the total assets on the balance sheet is a matter for the general meeting (art. 160.f LSC).
  - Set the approved **budget, minimum return, calendar and financing**.
  - Set **control milestones and exit or renegotiation criteria**. Examples: licence not granted within X months; pre-sales below 30 % within Y months; construction tender more than 8 % over budget.
- **Acquiring the plot:**
  1. Final checks on signing day: that day's *nota simple*, IBI and debt certificates.
  2. Public deed of sale.
  3. Tax returns: ITP or AJD on your community's form 600, within its deadline (usually 30 working days); IVA charged by the seller if the sale is subject to IVA.
  4. Registration at the Land Registry.
  5. Cancellation or regularisation of the agreed encumbrances, and actual taking of possession.

# Technical design

> **Objective:** have a design that gets the licence, can be built at the planned price and is accepted by the ten-year insurer.
> **Duration:** 110 min · **Result:** basic design and construction design, with the health and safety study, geotechnical study and waste management.

## Laws that apply

| Rule | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|
| LOE (Ley 38/1999), arts. 4-16 | What a design is and what each party does. For housing, the designer and the works director are **architects** (art. 10.2.a and 12.3.a) |
| CTE (RD 314/2006, amended by RD 732/2019 and RD 450/2022) | Technical requirements: DB SE, SI, SUA, HE, HR, HS |
| Structural Code (Código Estructural, RD 470/2021) | Concrete and steel |
| NCSE-02 (RD 997/2002) | Earthquakes. Especially in Granada, Murcia, Alicante, Málaga, Almería |
| RD 1000/2010 | **Compulsory** endorsement by the professional body (*visado*) of the construction design of a building (and of the final completion certificate) |
| RD 1627/1997 | Health and safety study or basic study |
| RD 105/2008 | Waste management study within the design |
| RD 390/2021 | Energy efficiency certificate of the design |
| Regional accessibility and habitability rules | Minimum areas, accessibility, sometimes stricter than the CTE |

## Where to go

| Institution | What you ask for | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Official Association of Architects** (*Colegio Oficial de Arquitectos*, Annex A) | List of architects, endorsement of the design | *Visado* (checks identity, qualification, insurance and that the design is complete) |
| **Association of Technical Architects** (*Colegio de Aparejadores y Arquitectos Técnicos*, Annex A) | Director of works execution and health and safety coordinator | Registered professionals and endorsement of their work |
| **Geotechnical laboratory** entered in the CCAA's register | Geotechnical study (CTE DB SE-C) | Type of foundation, water table, allowable bearing pressures |
| **OCT** | Review of the design | D0 report for the ten-year insurer |
| **Ten-year insurer** (through a broker) | Pre-review of the risk | Insurance terms before the design is closed |

## Lesson 3.1 — The LOE and the technical team

| Party | LOE article | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| Developer | 9 | Decides, finances, commissions. Takes out the insurance. Jointly liable with the other parties towards the buyers (art. 17.3) |
| Designer | 10 | Writes the design. For housing: an architect |
| Contractor | 11 | Builds according to the design and the instructions of the site supervision team |
| Works director | 12 | Directs the technical development. For housing: an architect |
| Director of works execution | 13 | Controls the physical execution and quality. For housing: a technical architect |
| Quality control bodies and laboratories | 14 | Tests and technical assistance |
| Product suppliers | 15 | Manufacturers and distributors |
| Owners and users | 16 | Maintain and use the building according to the manual |

- **Hire each professional in writing:** scope, fees, deadlines, minimum site visits, professional liability (RC) insurance.
- ⚠️ **Typical mistake:** buying a "cheap" design and then paying the difference in variations during the works.

## Lesson 3.2 — The Building Code (CTE)

| Basic document | What it governs |
|---|---|
| DB SE (+ SE-AE, SE-C, SE-A, SE-F, SE-M) | Structural safety, actions, foundations, steel, masonry, timber |
| DB SI | Fire: evacuation, fire resistance, installations |
| DB SUA | Safety in use and accessibility. SUA 9: an accessible lift when more than two storeys must be climbed from the accessible entrance or there are more than 12 homes on storeys without an accessible entrance |
| DB HE | Energy: HE0 consumption, HE1 envelope, HE2 thermal installations (RITE), HE3 lighting, HE4 renewables for domestic hot water (ACS), HE5 minimum renewable power generation in large new buildings (threshold of 1.000 m² built; check with your designer whether your building falls in), HE6 electric vehicle charging infrastructure |
| DB HR | Noise |
| DB HS | Health and hygiene: damp, waste, air quality, water, drainage, radon (HS6) |

- **Outside the CTE but compulsory:** Structural Code, NCSE-02 (earthquakes), REBT, RITE, ICT, RIPCI.
- **Radon (HS6):** compulsory in the municipalities listed in appendix B of DB HS. Check it before designing the basement.

## Lesson 3.3 — Design stages

1. **Preliminary study / outline design** (2-4 weeks): massing, unit types, number of homes. Not endorsed. Used to settle the mix with the sales team.
2. **Basic design** (4-8 weeks): defines the building and shows compliance with the planning rules and the basic requirements of the CTE. Most municipalities grant the licence on it (some ask for the construction design). Endorsing the basic design is voluntary unless the town hall requires it.
3. **Construction design** (a further 6-12 weeks): full construction detail. **Compulsory endorsement** (RD 1000/2010). It is what you contract with the builder and what you need to start the works.
- **Contents of the construction design:**
  - Descriptive and construction report, and report of compliance with the CTE
  - Drawings: plans, elevations, sections, structure, building services, details
  - Specifications
  - Measurements and budget (PEM)
  - Geotechnical study (appendix)
  - Waste management study (RD 105/2008)
  - Health and safety study or basic study (RD 1627/1997)
  - ICT design (RD 346/2011), signed by a telecommunications engineer or qualified professional
  - Energy efficiency certificate of the design (RD 390/2021)
  - Quality control plan
- **Tip:** before endorsement, ask the OCT and two contractors to review the design. Every mistake you find there costs ten times less than on site.

- **Surveys and technical studies before the outline design:**
  - **Georeferenced** topographic survey (it also helps you align Cadastre and Land Registry, Ley 13/2015).
  - Geotechnical study of the ground.
  - Investigation of contamination, made ground or buried services where relevant.
  - Inspection of existing buildings and demolition plan.
  - Enquiries to the utility companies about water, sewerage, electricity, gas and telecoms networks.
  - Access, levels, drainage and conditions of the surroundings.
- **Brief:** number of homes and areas by unit type, orientation, views, privacy, energy efficiency, garages, storerooms, lifts, courtyards and common areas.
- **Design to target cost:** fit the design to the sale prices and to the cost in the CRP before moving on to the basic design.
- **At basic design stage,** ask the sectoral bodies for their preliminary reports where needed.
- **At construction design stage,** coordinate architecture, structure, electrical, HVAC, plumbing and drainage (with BIM if you can) and prepare measurements, budget and tender documents.

## Lesson 3.4 — Health and safety in the design

| Document | When | Who writes it |
|---|---|---|
| **Health and safety study** | PEM ≥ 450.759,08 €, or more than 30 working days with more than 20 workers at the same time, or more than 500 worker-days, or tunnels, galleries, dams | A qualified professional appointed by the developer |
| **Basic health and safety study** | All other works | A qualified professional |
| **Health and safety plan** | Always, before starting | Each contractor. Approved by the coordinator during the works (or by the site supervision team if there is no coordinator) |

- **Health and safety coordinator:**
  - At design stage: compulsory if several designers take part.
  - During the works: compulsory if more than one company takes part, or a company and self-employed workers, or several self-employed workers. On a development, always.
  - Appointed by the developer. Keeps the **incidents book**.
- **Liability:** a serious accident can end in criminal liability (arts. 316-318 of the Criminal Code). Appoint the coordinator in writing and check that they visit the site.

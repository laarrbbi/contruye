## Module 1 checklist

- [ ] Product sheet defined (type, buyers, unit mix, specification, target price)
- [ ] Up-to-date nota simple and certificate of encumbrances
- [ ] Cadastral reference and topographic survey match
- [ ] Written planning report or certificate (informe o cédula urbanística) from the town hall
- [ ] Massing study from the architect
- [ ] Certificate of no IBI or local-tax debts
- [ ] Preliminary geotechnical study if there are basements
- [ ] Utility connection capacity confirmed with the companies
- [ ] Flood risk and heritage protection checked
- [ ] Sectoral easements reviewed (watercourses, coast, roads, heritage)
- [ ] Preliminary CRP with the maximum land price (Module 2)
- [ ] SPE incorporated
- [ ] Option to buy or deed signed and registered

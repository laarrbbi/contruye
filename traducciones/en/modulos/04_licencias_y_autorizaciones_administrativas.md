# Licences and administrative authorisations

> **Objective:** get the building licence in the shortest possible time and open the site without breaching anything.
> **Duration:** 100 min · **Result:** licence granted, ICIO paid, workplace opening notified and setting-out record signed.

## Laws that apply

| Rule | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|
| TRLSRU (RDL 7/2015), art. 11.4 | New-build works need an express decision. **No decision, no licence: silence is negative** |
| Regional planning law (Annex B) | Type of permit (licence or responsible declaration), documents, deadlines to decide and expiry |
| Ley 39/2015, on the Common Administrative Procedure | Correction within 10 days (art. 68), appeals (reconsideration: 1 month; judicial review: 2 months) |
| RDL 2/2004 (TRLRHL), arts. 100-103 | ICIO: up to 4 %, due when the works start; provisional and final assessment on the actual cost |
| Ley 21/2013, on environmental assessment | Urban projects above the thresholds of annex II, or those set by the CCAA |
| RD 849/1986 (Public Water Domain) | A 100 m control zone on each side of a watercourse |
| Ley 22/1988, on Coasts | Protection easement (100 m; 20 m on land that was urban in 1988) and right-of-way easement |
| Ley 37/2015 (roads) and Ley 38/2015 (railways) | Protection zones and building line |
| Ley 48/1960 and RD 297/2013 (aeronautical easements) | Maximum heights near airports: AESA report |
| Ley 16/1985 + regional heritage law | Listed assets of cultural interest and archaeological zones |
| RD 1627/1997, art. 19 + Orden TIN/1071/2010 | Notification of the opening of the workplace |
| Ley 32/2006 + RD 1109/2007 | Register of Accredited Companies (REA) and subcontracting book |

## Where to go

| Institution | What you ask for | Indicative time |
|---|---|---|
| **Town hall · Planning** (e-office) | Major works licence (or responsible declaration where the law allows) | 1-6 months depending on the municipality and the regional law |
| **Town hall · Tax office** | Self-assessment or provisional assessment of the ICIO and the fee | With the application or the grant, per by-law |
| **River basin authority** (*Confederación Hidrográfica*) or regional water agency (Annex A) | Authorisation in the control zone | 2-6 months |
| **Coastal authority** (provincial *Demarcación* or *Servicio de Costas*, MITECO) and CCAA | Report and authorisation in the protection easement | 2-6 months |
| **State Roads Authority** / CCAA / provincial council (*Diputación*) | Authorisation in a road protection zone | 1-3 months |
| **ADIF** | Authorisation in a railway protection zone | 1-3 months |
| **AESA** | Aeronautical easements report | 1-3 months |
| **CCAA's culture department** | Heritage report or archaeological intervention | 1-3 months |
| **CCAA's labour authority** (Annex A) | Opening notification (filed by the contractor), REA, validation of the subcontracting book | Before starting |
| **Association of Architects / of Technical Architects** | Orders and attendance book, incidents book | At the start |

## Lesson 4.1 — The building licence

- **First, the type of control:** confirm which permit applies under the regional law and the by-law (licence, responsible declaration or prior notification) and whether you need **separate licences** for demolition, earthworks, urbanisation works or tree felling.

- **Which permit you need:** for new residential building, a **major works licence** (*licencia de obras mayor*). Some regional laws allow a responsible declaration in specific cases; for new build the rule is still an express licence (art. 11.4 TRLSRU).
- **Step by step:**
  1. Preliminary meeting with the municipal officer, with the outline design. Ask which sectoral reports they will require.
  2. File through the e-office: application, design (basic or construction, depending on the town hall), health and safety study, waste management study, any sectoral authorisations you already have, proof of payment of fees and ICIO if the by-law requires it on application, waste deposit if the by-law asks for one.
  3. The town hall checks that the file is complete. If something is missing it sends you a request, and you have **10 days** to correct it.
  4. Technical report (municipal architect) and legal report.
  5. Decision. If favourable, the licence sets the start and completion deadlines and conditions.
  6. If refused: appeal for reconsideration (1 month, optional) or judicial review (2 months).
- **Administrative silence:** on new build **it is negative** (art. 11.4 TRLSRU). If the deadline passes with no decision, you cannot start.
- **ICIO:**
  - Base: the actual cost of the physical execution. It does not include IVA, fees, professional fees or the contractor's profit (art. 102.1 TRLRHL).
  - Rate: the by-law's, 4 % at most. Possible rebates (up to 95 %) for works of special interest, solar energy, accessibility or protected housing, if the by-law provides for them.
  - It falls due when the works start. Provisional assessment when the licence is applied for or granted, and final assessment at completion, on the actual cost.
- ⚠️ **Typical mistakes:** filing an incomplete design (every request adds weeks); not asking about sectoral reports beforehand; starting on silence, thinking it is positive.

## Lesson 4.2 — Sectoral authorisations

- **How to know which ones you need:** check in the PGOU viewer and the sectoral viewers (river basin authority, Coasts, roads, AESA, heritage) whether the plot touches any protection zone. Apply in parallel: do not wait for the town hall to ask for them.

| If the plot is… | You need |
|---|---|
| Less than 100 m from a river or ravine | Authorisation from the river basin authority or regional water agency |
| In the coastal protection strip | Authorisation from the CCAA and a Coasts report |
| Next to a road | Authorisation from its owner (State, CCAA or *Diputación*) |
| Next to a railway line | Authorisation from ADIF |
| Near an airport (heights) | AESA report |
| In an archaeological zone or with a BIC | Report or archaeological intervention by the culture department |
| Above environmental thresholds | Environmental impact assessment (the CCAA's environmental body) |
| Near a military installation | Authorisation from the Ministry of Defence |

- **Complementary steps so you can work:**
  - **Utility connections:** supply and connection-point applications to the electricity distributor, the water company and the other companies.
  - **Use of the public highway:** hoardings, scaffolding, skips and storage pay a municipal fee.
  - **Tower crane:** installation project, erection by an authorised company and registration with the community's Industry Service (ITC MIE-AEM 2, RD 836/2003). Some town halls also require authorisation for swinging over the public highway.
  - **Road closures** for concrete pours or deliveries: municipal authorisation and local police.
  - **Outstanding urbanisation works:** approved urbanisation project; many town halls require a guarantee if you urbanise while you build.
  - Authorisations for the installations that need them.

## Lesson 4.3 — Opening the site legally

- **Order of steps before the first lorry:**
  1. Licence granted and its conditions read (deposits, site board, deadlines).
  2. **Health and safety coordinator** appointed in writing by the developer.
  3. The contractor's **health and safety plan** approved by the coordinator.
  4. **Notification of the opening of the workplace** to the CCAA's labour authority: filed by the contractor, with the health and safety plan, before starting.
  5. Contractor and subcontractors entered in the **REA**.
  6. **Subcontracting book** validated by the labour authority.
  7. **Orders and attendance book** (site supervision team) and **incidents book** (coordinator).
  8. **Setting-out verification record** (*acta de comprobación del replanteo*) signed by the works director, the director of works execution and the contractor. It sets the start date of the construction period.
  9. **Site board** as the by-law requires (licence number, developer, professionals, contractor, deadlines).
  10. Notification of the start to the town hall if the by-law asks for it.
- **Deed declaring the new building under construction** (Module 9): you need it so the bank can register the mortgage on the future building.
- ⚠️ **Typical mistake:** starting the excavation before the opening notification. It is a serious breach of health and safety rules.

- **Organise the site supervision team before starting:** appoint in writing the architect as works director, the technical architect as director of works execution, the health and safety coordinator, the specialist professionals and the quality control, with the scope of each.

## Lesson 4.4 — Deadlines, extensions and expiry

| Item | Rule of thumb |
|---|---|
| Start deadline | The one set by the licence or the regional law (usually 6 months to 1 year) |
| Completion deadline | The licence's (usually 2-3 years) |
| Extension | Ask for it in writing **before** it runs out. It is usually granted once |
| Expiry | It is not automatic: the town hall opens a procedure in which you are heard. Once declared, you need a new licence |

- **Tip:** apply for the licence when your financing is on track. A licence that expires in a falling market is an enormous cost.

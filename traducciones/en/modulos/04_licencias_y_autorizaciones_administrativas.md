## Module 4 checklist

- [ ] Preliminary meeting with the planning department
- [ ] Sectoral authorisations identified and applied for in parallel
- [ ] Complete application (design, ESS, waste, fees, deposits)
- [ ] Licence granted and its conditions reviewed
- [ ] ICIO paid
- [ ] Coordinator appointed and safety plan approved
- [ ] Opening notification, REA and subcontracting book
- [ ] Orders and incidents books
- [ ] Demolition, earthworks or tree-felling licences if they apply
- [ ] Use of the public highway, crane and road closures authorised
- [ ] Setting-out record signed and site board put up

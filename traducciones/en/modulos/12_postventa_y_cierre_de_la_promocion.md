# After-sales and closing the development

> **Objective:** handle warranty claims in an orderly way and close the deal financially, for tax and at company level.
> **Duration:** 90 min · **Result:** after-sales service running, final account settled with the contractor, taxes closed and SPE wound up or kept in reserve.

## Laws that apply

| Rule | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|
| LOE, arts. 17 and 18 | Warranty periods and **limitation: 2 years** from when the damage occurs to bring a claim |
| LOE, art. 17.3 and 18.2 | The developer is liable towards the buyer and can recover from the party responsible |
| Ley 27/2014 (IS), arts. 16 and 29 | Limit on deducting finance costs (30 % of operating profit, with a minimum of 1 M€) and rates |
| Ley 37/1992 (IVA) | Returns, and the adjustment if you rent out unsold homes (renting a home is exempt) |
| TRLRHL, arts. 104-110 + RDL 26/2021 | Plusvalía: objective or actual method; no increase in value, no tax |
| RDL 1/2010 (LSC), arts. 360-400 | Dissolution, liquidation and extinction of the SPE; art. 399: after extinction, the shareholders are liable up to what they received |
| General Accounting Plan + the sector adaptation for real estate companies | Developments are **inventory** |

## Where to go

| Institution | What you do |
|---|---|
| **Contractor and other parties** | Warranty repairs, charged to the retentions if they do not respond |
| **Ten-year insurer** | Claims for structural damage |
| **AEAT or foral tax authority** | IS (form 200), IVA (303 and 390), deregistration (036) |
| **Companies Registry** | Filing of accounts, dissolution, extinction |
| **Notary** | Deeds cancelling the mortgage and extinguishing the company |
| **CCAA's Consumer Arbitration Board** (*Junta Arbitral de Consumo*) | Arbitration of buyers' claims |

## Lesson 12.1 — Handing over to the owners' association

- Hand over to the chair or the property manager:
  - The Building Book.
  - Documentation of the common areas and installations.
  - Maintenance contracts in force (lift, PCI, doors, landscaping).
  - The initial reserve fund, if you have provided one.
  - Keys and remotes for the common areas.
- Sign a handover record.

- **Organise the maintenance from the start** for the lifts, equipment, pool, gardens and other common services, and hand over the minute book (it is validated at the Land Registry, art. 19 LPH).

## Lesson 12.2 — The after-sales service

- **Protocol:**
  1. A single channel (form or email) with an incident number.
  2. Response within 48-72 h (good practice, not a legal deadline).
  3. Technical visit and classification by **nature** (finishing 1 year, habitability 3 years, structure 10 years, or misuse) and by **urgency** (urgent within 24 h if there are leaks, a loss of supply or a risk to people).
  4. Instruction to the contractor or the trade responsible, in writing, with photos and a deadline.
  5. If they do not respond: repair it yourself and charge the cost on (retention).
  6. Close-out signed by the owner.
- **Most frequent incidents:**

| Type | Usual cause |
|---|---|
| Damp in ceilings or walls | Roof, terraces, sealants |
| Doors and windows | Adjustments, settlement |
| HVAC and domestic hot water (ACS) | Commissioning, bleeding |
| Non-structural cracks | Normal shrinkage and settlement |
| Damp in basements | Waterproofing |
| Condensation | Insufficient ventilation or misuse |

- **Structural damage:** notify the ten-year insurer as soon as you know.
- **Limitation:** the buyer has 2 years from when the damage appears to bring a claim (art. 18 LOE). You have the same period, from when you pay, to recover from the party responsible.

- **Warranty periods and claim periods are not the same thing:**
  - Warranty (art. 17.1 LOE): 1, 3 and 10 years from handover of the works without reservations, or from when the reservations are remedied.
  - Limitation of the action (art. 18 LOE): 2 years from when the damage occurs.
  - On top of that there are contractual warranties and the action for breach of contract (art. 1964 CC: 5 years).

## Lesson 12.3 — Financial and tax close

- **Final account with the contractor:** approve the final valuation and settle the contract (retentions, penalties, extras). Release the retention when its period ends.
- **Close the accounts** with professionals, suppliers and vendors, and settle outstanding invoices and warranties.
- **Reconcile** sales revenue, costs, interest and overheads.
- **Actual return:** compare it with the approved CRP (deviations in cost, time and sales). Run the FINANCIALS model again with the actual figures.

- **Accounting:** every cost has been capitalised as **inventory** (development in progress → finished building). At each deed the sale is recognised and the proportional cost is written off.
- **Finance costs:** if the development lasts more than a year, the interest on the development loan is capitalised as an additional cost of the inventory.
- **Corporate income tax (IS):**
  - 2026 rates: general 25 %; newly created 15 %; small company 23 %; micro-company 19 % / 21 % (Ley 7/2024).
  - Limit on finance costs (art. 16 LIS).
  - Annual return (form 200) and payments on account (form 202).
  - In Navarra and País Vasco, foral rules.
- **IVA:** quarterly (303) and annual summary (390). If you rent out homes you have not sold, adjust the IVA you deducted (renting a home is exempt).
- **Municipal plusvalía:** one self-assessment for each sale. Choose the more favourable method (objective or actual). If there is no increase in value, there is no tax.
- **IBI:** on the homes that are still yours on 1 January.

## Lesson 12.4 — Closing the SPE

- **Before winding up:**
  1. Full cancellation of the development loan at the Land Registry.
  2. Final account with the contractor: retentions, penalties, extras.
  3. Payment of every supplier and professional.
  4. Taxes up to date.
- **Dissolution and liquidation:**
  1. General meeting: resolution to dissolve and appointment of a liquidator.
  2. Final balance sheet and distribution among the shareholders.
  3. Deed of extinction before a notary.
  4. Registration at the Companies Registry.
  5. Deregistration with the AEAT (or the foral tax authority).
- **Beware of ten-year liability:** extinction does not erase the warranties. The shareholders are liable up to what they received in the liquidation (art. 399 LSC). Many developers keep the SPE dormant until the periods have run, or reuse it for the next development.
- **Lessons learned:** a closing meeting with the team: what worked, what did not, what changes next time.

- **Administrative close:**
  - Archive the development's **complete file**: designs, licences, certificates, contracts, invoices and warranties.
  - Minimum retention periods: accounts and invoices 6 years (Commercial Code, art. 30); tax documents for as long as they can be audited (4 years, LGT art. 66, or longer if there are tax losses or deductions carried forward); technical and warranty documents at least 10 years plus the 2 years of limitation (LOE arts. 17-18).
  - Close temporary contracts (construction insurance, site utilities, cabins, rentals).
  - Meet the company's accounting, tax and corporate obligations.
  - Keep the documents available for inspections and claims.
  - Review the deviations in cost, time and sales for the next development.

# Construction

> **Objective:** understand how a residential building is built, in what order and which rules govern each part.
> **Duration:** 150 min · **Result:** works finished, final completion certificate endorsed and handover record signed.

## Laws that apply

| Part of the works | Rule |
|---|---|
| Waste and soil | RD 105/2008 · Ley 7/2022 · Orden APM/1007/2017 (reuse of soil) |
| Foundations and structure | CTE DB SE · Structural Code (Código Estructural, RD 470/2021) · NCSE-02 |
| Façades and roofs | CTE DB HE1, HS1, HR, SI |
| Electrical | REBT (RD 842/2002), ITC-BT-25 in homes, ITC-BT-52 electric vehicle charging (RD 1053/2014) + CTE DB HE6 (updated by RD 450/2022) |
| Thermal and domestic hot water (ACS) | RITE (RD 1027/2007) + CTE DB HE2 and HE4 |
| Gas | RD 919/2006 |
| Telecoms | ICT (RD 346/2011) |
| Fire | CTE DB SI + RIPCI (RD 513/2017) |
| Lifts | RD 203/2016 (CE marking) + RD 88/2013 (ITC AEM 1) |
| Water and drainage | CTE DB HS4 and HS5 + the water company's by-law |
| Handover | LOE, art. 6 |

## Lesson 6.1 — Earthworks, foundations and structure

- **Preliminary work:** hoardings, site cabins, site connections, topographic setting-out, protection of party walls, diversion of services, demolition (with asbestos removed by a company entered in the RERA if there is any).
- **Earthworks:**
  - Basement excavation. Diaphragm walls or retaining walls if there are party walls.
  - Groundwater: pumping or drainage. It is the great source of cost overruns.
  - Soil: reuse it if it is clean (Orden APM/1007/2017) or take it to an authorised manager. Keep the certificates.
- **Foundations** (defined by the geotechnical engineer and designed by the structural engineer):
  - Pad or strip footings with tie beams: good ground.
  - Raft: soft or uneven ground, or basements with water.
  - Piles: good ground at depth.
  - Micropiles: tight spaces or underpinning.
- **Structure:** reinforced concrete cast in situ (the usual), with waffle slabs, one-way slabs or flat slabs. Composite with steel, or precast, to save time.
- **Control:** the director of works execution checks the reinforcement and the pours. An accredited laboratory tests the concrete (test cubes, strength at 28 days) and the steel under the Structural Code. The OCT inspects for the insurer.
- ⚠️ **Typical mistakes:** not having enough geotechnical information before excavating; pouring concrete without the reinforcement check; losing the concrete delivery notes and certificates (the OCT asks for them).

- **Do not forget:** clearing and cleaning the plot before excavating, and having the geotechnical engineer approve the formation level before laying the foundations.

## Lesson 6.2 — Façades, windows and roofs

- **Façade:** it must meet DB HE1 (thermal transmittance), HS1 (damp), HR (noise) and SI (external fire spread).
  - Brick + insulation + internal lining.
  - SATE (external thermal insulation system).
  - Ventilated façade.
- **Windows:** PVC or thermally broken aluminium, low-emissivity double or triple glazing. Check the values required for your climate zone.
- **Roof:** flat (waterproofing + insulation + protection) or pitched. **Watertightness test** before finishing: flooding or spraying, with a record.
- **Waterproofing of basements and roofs:** reviewed by the OCT. It is the most frequent after-sales claim.

- **Thermal bridges and construction junctions:** check them on site; they cause a lot of condensation claims after sale.
- **Watertightness tests** on façades and windows as well as roofs and terraces, and a check of the insulation.

## Lesson 6.3 — Building services

- They account for 20–30 % of the PEM. Coordinate them from the design stage (BIM helps).
- **Water and drainage:** CTE HS4 and HS5, separate system if the municipality has one.
- **Domestic hot water (ACS) and HVAC:**
  - CTE HE4: minimum renewable contribution for ACS (60 %, or 70 % for large consumption). Aerothermal heat pumps count as renewable.
  - RITE: design or technical report, installer's certificate and registration with Industry.
  - Today the norm is **aerothermal heat pumps** for ACS, heating and cooling, with no gas.
- **Electrical (REBT):**
  - ITC-BT-25: basic (5.750 W) or high (9.200 W) electrification.
  - Centralised meters, main supply line, individual feeders.
  - **Electric vehicle charging:** ITC-BT-52 and CTE HE6 require, in new residential buildings with parking, pre-installation (cable ducts) for **every space**.
  - Electrical installation certificate (CIE) from the authorised installer, registered with Industry.
- **Gas** (if any): RD 919/2006.
- **Telecoms (ICT):** RITI and RITS, fibre-optic, coaxial and twisted-pair networks up to each home. ICT completion certificate and installer's certificate.
- **Fire protection:** DB SI + RIPCI. CO detection and ventilation in the garage, extinguishers, fire hose reels (BIE) according to floor area. Qualified installer.
- **Lifts:** CE marking, authorised maintenance company, registration with Industry.

- **Renewable energy:** photovoltaic or other systems when CTE HE5 requires it or the design provides for it.
- **Garage extraction, earthing and control systems** complete the building services.

## Lesson 6.4 — Finishes and external works

- Partitions (ceramic or plasterboard), floors (porcelain tile, parquet), wall tiling, paint, internal joinery, wardrobes, kitchen (units and, depending on the segment, appliances).
- Common areas: entrance hall, stairs, lift, gardens, pool (regional rules for communal pools if they apply).
- Garage: floor finish, signage, ventilation, CO detection, automatic door.
- External works, and handing over to the town hall whatever becomes public domain, if the licence requires it.
- **Tip:** freeze the specification in the buyers' contract. Every sales change halfway through the works is a variation.

- **Common areas and internal external works:**
  - Paving of accesses, pavements and internal roads.
  - Landscaping, irrigation and outdoor furniture.
  - **Communal pool:** technical and health criteria of RD 742/2013 and the regional pools decree.
  - **Children's play areas:** standards UNE-EN 1176 and 1177 (equipment and surfacing).
  - Plot boundaries, external lighting, permanent connections and the associated urbanisation works.

## Lesson 6.5 — Completion and handover

- **Final completion certificate (CFO):** signed by the works director and the director of works execution; it certifies that the works were built according to the design. **Compulsory endorsement** (RD 1000/2010).
- **Handover record (art. 6 LOE):**
  - Signed by the developer and the contractor (and the site supervision team).
  - Within 30 days of the completion date in the CFO. If there is neither a record nor a reasoned rejection within that period, handover is deemed tacit.
  - Without reservations, with reservations (snagging list and deadline) or reasoned rejection.
  - **The 1, 3 and 10-year warranties run from handover without reservations, or from when the reservations are remedied** (art. 17.1 LOE).
- **Documentation of the works as built (art. 7 LOE):** the works director hands it to the developer (design with its changes, list of parties, use and maintenance instructions, certificates). It is the basis of the Building Book (Module 9).
- **Ask the contractor for:** as-built drawings, equipment warranties, installers' certificates, waste management certificates, manuals.

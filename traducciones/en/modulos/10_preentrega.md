# Pre-handover

> **Objective:** every buyer receives a home without defects, and the notary has everything ready before signing day.
> **Duration:** 70 min · **Result:** snagging lists closed, handover documentation for each home and signing calendar.

## Laws that apply

| Rule | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|
| LOE, art. 17 | Whatever is not snagged now comes back later as a warranty claim |
| TRLGDCU, art. 89.3 | Costs you cannot pass on to the buyer |
| Notarial Regulation (*Reglamento Notarial*), art. 126 | The consumer buyer chooses the notary |
| Ley 5/2019, arts. 14 and 15 | The buyer's mortgage: the bank gives them the documents (FEIN, FiAE) at least 10 calendar days beforehand, and the buyer appears before the notary for the preliminary record at least the day before signing |
| Ley 10/2010 | Identification and origin of funds before signing |

## Where to go

| Who | What you ask for |
|---|---|
| **Contractor** | Snags fixed on time (charged to the retention if it does not comply) |
| **Notary** | Signing calendar, draft deed, provision for costs |
| **The developer's bank** | Debt certificate for each home and redemption or take-over letter |
| **The buyers' banks** | Signing date for each mortgage |
| **Town hall** | Cadastral values and data for each home's plusvalía |

## Lesson 10.1 — Snagging

- **Round 1 · internal (6-8 weeks before):** the developer's professional + the site manager go through each home with a snagging sheet per room. The contractor fixes.
- **Round 2 · buyer (3-4 weeks before):** guided visit, the buyer notes their defects, the pre-handover sheet is signed with a deadline for fixing.
- **Round 3 · verification:** final check before signing.
- **What to check:**

| Area | Points |
|---|---|
| Finishes | Knocks on walls and ceilings, paint, floor joints, scratches on floors and glass |
| Internal joinery | Doors closing, ironmongery, wardrobes |
| External joinery | Windows closing and watertight, shutters, sealants |
| Electrical | Every light point and socket, RCDs, video entry phone |
| Plumbing | Pressure, hot water, drains, traps, no leaks |
| HVAC | Aerothermal heat pump, splits or underfloor heating working |
| Damp | Ceilings under terraces and roof, basements |
| Telecoms | Sockets and fibre in each room |
| Common areas | Lift, lighting, garage doors, landscaping |

- **Tip:** use an app or a sheet with photos and close each point with a date and a signature.

## Lesson 10.2 — Documentation for each home

| Document | Note |
|---|---|
| Copy of the deed (after signing) | The notary provides it |
| Ten-year policy certificate | Policy number and insurer |
| Equipment warranties | Aerothermal heat pump, appliances if any |
| Use and maintenance manual | Extract from the Building Book |
| Plans of the home | With areas and the position of the consumer unit and stopcocks |
| Energy efficiency certificate and label | For the finished building |
| Certificate of habitability | In the CCAA that require it |
| Owners' association details | Statutes, share, property manager |
| Utility details | Electricity CUPS, water meter, CIE |
| Sets of keys and remotes | Home, entrance, storeroom, garage |

- **For each buyer, before calling them in:** confirm they have made the payments under the contract, and review the position on payments, taxes and costs that fall to each party.
- **Complete the handover file** with: registry identification of the home and its appurtenances; access cards and codes; the association's rules, property manager's details and expected service charges; inspection record with incidents and commitments to fix them.
- **During the visit,** explain how the equipment, HVAC, ventilation and home automation (if any) work.

## Lesson 10.3 — The notary

- **3-4 weeks before:**
  - The buyer chooses the notary (a consumer right). Offer yours, without imposing it.
  - Send each notary: new building and horizontal division registered, licence or declaration of first occupation, cédula if it applies, ten-year insurance, energy certificate, up-to-date *nota simple* of the property, certificate from the owners' association that it is up to date.
  - Ask the bank for the **debt certificate** for each property and the redemption letter to cancel its share (or the take-over documents if the buyer chooses that).
  - Prepare the **settlement statement** for each buyer: price, payments received with IVA, amount outstanding.
- **The buyer brings:** DNI or NIE, documents on the origin of the funds, proof of earlier payments, and their mortgage, which is signed the same day (with the documents received at least 10 calendar days beforehand and the notary's preliminary record done at least the day before, Ley 5/2019).
- **Means of payment:** the notary identifies each payment (named cheque or bank transfer). Nothing in cash above the legal limit.

## Module 10 checklist

- [ ] Three snagging rounds closed
- [ ] Handover documentation prepared for each home
- [ ] Notaries and dates confirmed
- [ ] Debt certificates and redemption letters from the bank
- [ ] Settlement statements for each buyer
- [ ] Money-laundering check on each buyer

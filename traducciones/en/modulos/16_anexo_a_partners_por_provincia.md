# ANNEX A — DIRECTORY OF PARTNERS BY PROVINCE AND BY PHASE

> **How to use it:** find your province. For each phase you have the institution or the type of partner, and what you need it for.
> The names of professional bodies and public agencies sometimes change: confirm them in the official search engines below before you go.

## Official search engines (valid throughout Spain)

| You need | Search engine |
|---|---|
| The plot's Land Registry (Registro de la Propiedad), notas simples | registradores.org |
| A notary | notariado.org |
| The Cadastre (except Navarra and País Vasco) | sede.catastro.gob.es |
| The Association of Architects (Colegio de Arquitectos) | cscae.com (Consejo Superior de Colegios de Arquitectos) |
| The Association of Technical Architects (Colegio de Aparejadores y Arquitectos Técnicos) | arquitectura-tecnica.com (CGATE) |
| The Association of Estate Agents (Colegio de Agentes de la Propiedad Inmobiliaria) | consejocoapis.org |
| The Association of Property Managers (Colegio de Administradores de Fincas) | cgcafe.org |
| The Association of Insurance Intermediaries (Colegio de Mediadores de Seguros) | consejo general de los colegios de mediadores de seguros |
| Approved valuation companies | Registro de entidades del Banco de España (bde.es) |
| Auctions | subastas.boe.es · the AEAT's electronic office · the Social Security's electronic office |
| State-owned public land | CASA47 (formerly SEPES) |
| Developers' associations | apce.es (Asociación de Promotores Constructores de España) |
| River basin authorities | MITECO, map of river basin districts |

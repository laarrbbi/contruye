# Legalisation

> **Objective:** turn the finished works into homes that can be conveyed, mortgaged and lived in.
> **Duration:** 105 min · **Result:** first occupation (and cédula where required), deed of completed new building and horizontal division registered, Building Book.

## Laws that apply

| Rule | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|
| **TRLSRU, art. 28** | Requirements for the deed and registration of a new building under construction and completed |
| **RD 1093/1997** | Rules for registering planning acts at the Land Registry |
| **LOE, arts. 7 and 20** | Building Book; ten-year insurance to execute the deed and register |
| **LPH (Ley 49/1960)**, art. 5 | Founding title of the horizontal property: shares and statutes |
| Regional planning and housing law | Licence or responsible declaration of first occupation; certificate of habitability where it still exists (Annex B) |
| **RD 390/2021** | Energy efficiency certificate of the finished building and the regional register |
| **TRLCI (RDL 1/2004)** | Entering the new building in the Cadastre |
| REBT, RITE, ICT, RIPCI | Certificates of the installations, registered |

## Where to go

| Institution | What you ask for | Indicative time |
|---|---|---|
| **Associations of Architects and Technical Architects** | Endorsement of the CFO | 1-2 weeks |
| **CCAA's Industry Service** | Registration of the installations (CIE, RITE, PCI, lift) | 1-4 weeks |
| **Regional register of energy certificates** | Registration of the finished building's certificate | 1-4 weeks |
| **Town hall** | Licence or responsible declaration of first occupation | 1-3 months (a responsible declaration takes effect at once, with a later check) |
| **Regional or island body** (in the 8 CCAA that still have it) | Certificate of habitability (*cédula de habitabilidad*) for each home | 1-2 months |
| **Notary** | Deed declaring the completed new building and the horizontal division | 1-3 weeks |
| **Land Registry** | Registration: each home becomes a property in its own right | 15 working days (extendable) |
| **Cadastre** | Entry of the new building (the notary and the registrar report it; if not, form 902-N) | 1-6 months |

## Lesson 9.1 — Technical documentation at completion

| Document | Who issues it |
|---|---|
| Final completion certificate (endorsed) | Works director + director of works execution |
| Energy efficiency certificate of the finished building (registered) | Qualified professional |
| Installation certificates registered with Industry | Authorised installers |
| ICT completion certificate and installer's certificate | Telecoms professional and installer |
| Final OCT report and final ten-year policy | OCT and insurer |
| Waste management certificates | Authorised manager |
| List of controls and tests | Director of works execution |
| As-built drawings | Site supervision team |

- **Final inspection of the works** (before applying for anything):
  - Go through every home, garage, storeroom and common area against the design, the licence and the specification.
  - Check that the building services, lifts and equipment work.
  - List the defects, snags and outstanding work, and track their close-out.
- **Tests and commissioning:**
  - Tests of the common and individual installations.
  - Certificates for the electrical, thermal and other regulated installations, with their inspection and registration.
  - Commissioning of the lift and the other equipment.
  - Permanent utility contracts and supplies.
  - Check of the building's safety, accessibility and operation.
- **Final documentation:** as-built drawings, certificates, tests, manuals, data sheets and manufacturers' warranties.

## Lesson 9.2 — New building and horizontal division

- **New building under construction** (at the start): licence + the professional's certificate that the description matches the design. It lets the bank take a mortgage over the future building.
- **Completed new building** (or completion record), art. 28 TRLSRU. You need:
  - The professional's final completion certificate.
  - Licence or verification of first occupation (or responsible declaration), depending on the regional law.
  - Ten-year insurance (art. 20 LOE).
  - Energy efficiency certificate.
  - Building Book, where the regional law requires it for registration.
- **Horizontal division (founding title, art. 5 LPH):**
  - Describes the building and each private unit (home, commercial unit, garage, storeroom) with number, floor, area, boundaries and **participation share**.
  - Common elements.
  - The owners' association's **statutes** (use of terraces, pool, sharing of costs, prohibited uses).
  - Normally in the same deed as the completed new building.
- **After registration:** each home is a separate registered property and can be sold and mortgaged on its own. The bank spreads the development loan's mortgage across the properties.
- **Cost:** notary and registry per official scale, AJD (Annex B) on the value of the new building and the horizontal division. **It cannot be passed on to a consumer buyer.**

- **Regularise** in the deed the areas, appurtenances and common elements if what was built differs from the original design, and check that the resulting properties match the drawings and titles.
- **Energy label:** get it and give it to the buyers; it is compulsory when offering and selling (RD 390/2021).

## Lesson 9.3 — First occupation and certificate of habitability

- **First occupation (town hall):** certifies that the building matches the licence and can be used.
  - Depending on the autonomous community it is a **licence** (express decision) or a **responsible declaration / prior notification** (immediate effect and later check). Madrid (Ley 1/2020), Andalucía (LISTA) and Galicia (Ley 2/2016) work with a responsible declaration or prior notification. See Annex B.
  - Usual documents: CFO, installation certificates, energy certificate, photos, Cadastre entry or proof of filing, certificate of the utility connections, final ICIO assessment.
- **Certificate of habitability (*cédula de habitabilidad*):** in 2026 it still exists in **Cataluña, Navarra, Canarias, Extremadura, Illes Balears, La Rioja, Asturias and Región de Murcia**. It is issued by the regional or island body and is usually needed to sign permanent utility contracts and to sell.
- ⚠️ **Typical mistakes:** applying for first occupation with unregistered installations; not having the permanent electricity connection done (the distributor takes months); forgetting the cédula in the communities that require it.

## Lesson 9.4 — The Building Book

- **Compulsory** (art. 7 LOE), and regulated in more detail by regional decrees.
- **Contents:**
  1. The design with its approved changes.
  2. List of the parties involved.
  3. Licences, insurance and warranties.
  4. Installation and test certificates.
  5. Use and maintenance instructions for the building and its installations.
  6. Maintenance plan with the compulsory inspections (lift, PCI, thermal, electrical).
- **Handing it over:** the developer gives it to the owners' association (chair or property manager). Each buyer gets the part for their home.

## Lesson 9.5 — Warranty file

- **Complete the ten-year insurance:** final policy after the OCT's final report.
- **Collect the warranties** for installations, equipment and materials (aerothermal heat pumps, lift, windows, waterproofing).
- **Document the liabilities** of each party to the building process: list of parties and their contracts (art. 7 LOE).
- **Prepare the warranty file** for the buyers and for the owners' association.
- **Define the claims procedure** that will apply after handover (Module 12).

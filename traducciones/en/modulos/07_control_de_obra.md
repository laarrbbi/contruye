- [ ] Quality control plan approved

- [ ] Programme updated every week
- [ ] Monthly report (cost, time, sales) sent to shareholders and the bank
- [ ] Non-conformity register up to date
- [ ] Budgeted, committed and actual cost reconciled
- [ ] Extensions of time documented

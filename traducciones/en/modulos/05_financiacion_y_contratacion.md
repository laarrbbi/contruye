## Module 5 checklist

- [ ] Valuation and binding offer from the bank
- [ ] Development loan and guarantee line signed
- [ ] Special account for advance payments opened
- [ ] Ten-year insurance (with OCT), TRC and RC taken out
- [ ] Tender with 3-5 contractors and comparison by chapter
- [ ] Construction contract signed with retentions, penalties and variations regime
- [ ] REA and the contractor's AEAT and TGSS certificates

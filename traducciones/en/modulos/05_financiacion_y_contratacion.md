# Financing and contracting

> **Objective:** finance the works, hire the right contractor and cover every risk with insurance and guarantees.
> **Duration:** 120 min · **Result:** development loan signed, insurance taken out, special account for buyers' advance payments opened and construction contract signed.

## Laws that apply

| Rule | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|
| LOE, **first additional provision** (*disposición adicional 1.ª*, as worded by Ley 20/2015) | Every amount you collect from a buyer before handover must be guaranteed by surety insurance or a joint and several bank guarantee and paid into a separate **special account** |
| LOE, art. 19 and **second additional provision** (*disposición adicional 2.ª*) | **Compulsory ten-year insurance** on residential buildings (exception: a self-builder of a single-family home for their own use) |
| LOE, art. 20 | Notaries and registrars do not authorise or register the completed new building without proof of the ten-year insurance |
| Orden ECO/805/2003 | Mortgage valuation of the project |
| Ley 5/2019 | The buyer's mortgage when they take over the development loan |
| Ley 32/2006 | Subcontracting: REA, at most three levels of subcontracting, subcontracting book |
| Workers' Statute (*Estatuto de los Trabajadores*), art. 42, and General Tax Act (*Ley General Tributaria*), art. 43.1.f | Liability for the contractor's Social Security and tax debts: ask for certificates that it is up to date |
| Ley 3/2004, on late payment | Maximum payment term between businesses: 60 days |
| Civil Code (*Código Civil*), arts. 1544 and 1588-1600 | The construction contract |

## Lesson 5.1 — The development loan

| Item | Benchmark 2025-2026 |
|---|---|
| Amount | 60–70 % of total cost (or of the finished valuation, depending on the bank) |
| Rate | 12-month Euríbor + 1,5–3 % |
| Arrangement fee | 0,5–1,5 % |
| Commitment fee | 0,1–0,5 % a year on the undrawn amount |
| Term | 24–48 months to handover, plus the take-over period |
| Drawdown | Against construction valuations |

- **What the bank requires:**
  - **Pre-sales:** 30–50 % of the homes with a private contract and an advance payment.
  - **Equity:** 30–40 % of the cost (land already paid for usually counts).
  - Approved valuation (finished-building hypothesis).
  - Mortgage over the land and the future building (deed of new building under construction).
  - Licence, construction design, construction contract, budget, programme and CRP.
  - Policies: ten-year, TRC, RC.
  - Personal guarantees from the shareholders, for small developers.
- **Drawdown:** the director of works execution certifies each month → the bank's valuer checks progress → the bank releases the amount → you pay the contractor.
- **Take-over (*subrogación*):** at the deed, the buyer can take on their share of the loan as their own mortgage (with the protections of Ley 5/2019). If they do not, that share is cancelled with the buyer's payment.
- ⚠️ **Typical mistakes:** not negotiating the cost of the guarantees within the loan; not planning the cash between valuation and drawdown (5-15 days); signing without a clause releasing each home from the mortgage.

## Lesson 5.2 — Insurance and guarantees

- **LOE warranties (art. 17)**, counted **from the date of handover of the works without reservations, or from when the reservations are remedied**:

| Period | What it covers | Who is liable |
|---|---|---|
| 1 year | Finishing defects | The contractor (and the developer is liable towards the buyer) |
| 3 years | Defects affecting habitability (building services, damp, insulation) | Every party according to their role; the developer jointly and severally |
| 10 years | Structural damage that compromises strength and stability | Every party according to their role; the developer jointly and severally |

- **Ten-year insurance (compulsory on housing):**
  - Taken out by the developer. Beneficiaries: the successive owners.
  - Minimum sum insured: the final cost of the works (fees included).
  - Maximum legal excess: 1 % of the sum insured.
  - The insurer requires an **OCT** from the design stage.
  - Without it there is no deed of completed new building and no registration.
  - Cost: 0,8–1,5 % of the construction cost.
- **Three-year and one-year insurance:** provided for in the LOE but not compulsory yet.
- **Contractors' all risks (TRC):** damage to the works during construction. Not a legal requirement; the bank requires it.
- **Developer's third-party liability:** damage to third parties during the works. Advisable, and normally required by the bank.
- **Guarantee of advance payments (DA 1.ª LOE):**
  - Compulsory for every euro you collect before handing over the home: reservation, signing and payments during the works.
  - By surety insurance or a joint and several guarantee from an authorised institution, **individual for each buyer**.
  - It covers what was paid **plus statutory interest**.
  - The money goes into a separate **special account**, available only for the construction.
  - If you do not finish, or do not get first occupation (or the cédula) on time, the buyer gets their money back with interest.
  - Collecting without a guarantee is a consumer-law infringement.

## Lesson 5.3 — Choosing a contractor

- **Tender documents:** construction design, measurements, specification, interim deadlines, payment terms, penalties, retentions, insurance required.
- **Invite 3-5 contractors** with:
  - Similar finished buildings (visit them and talk to the developers).
  - Annual accounts for the last three years (Companies Registry).
  - Entry in the REA.
  - Certificates that they are up to date with the AEAT and Social Security.
  - Their own team: site manager, foreman, production.
  - State contractor classification: not compulsory on private works, but a good sign of solvency.
- **Comparison by chapter:** never compare the total alone. Look for underpriced or missing items.
- **Warning sign:** a bid 15-20 % below the average usually means mistakes, worse materials or a company that will run out of cash.

- **In each bid, also review:** exclusions, own resources, and the company's employment, health and safety, tax and insurance documents.
- **Performance guarantee:** on top of the retention, you can ask for a performance bond (usually 5–10 % of the price).

## Lesson 5.4 — The construction contract

- **Types:**
  - **Fixed price (turnkey):** certainty of cost. It needs a very well-defined design.
  - **By units of work (measurements):** you pay for what is actually built. More risk of overrun.
  - **Mixed:** the usual one.
- **Clauses you cannot do without:**
  1. Purpose: the endorsed construction design as a contract document.
  2. Price, price review (if any) and valuation calendar.
  3. Payment at 60 days at most (Ley 3/2004).
  4. **Retention:** 5 % of each valuation until one year after handover, or an equivalent guarantee.
  5. Total period from the setting-out record, and intermediate milestones.
  6. **Delay penalties** (e.g. 0,05–0,2 % of the price per day, with a cap).
  7. Subcontracting: list agreed beforehand, REA compulsory, three levels at most, AEAT and TGSS certificates for each subcontractor.
  8. The contractor's insurance: RC, accidents, and its share of the TRC.
  9. Quality control and acceptance of the OCT and the laboratory.
  10. Variations: only with a written order and a price agreed **before** the work is done.
  11. Waste management (the contractor's plan).
  12. Handover, final documentation (as-built drawings, warranties, manuals) and snagging.
  13. Termination for breach and taking possession of the works.
- ⚠️ **Typical mistakes:** contracting on an incomplete design at a fixed price (every gap becomes a variation); not asking for AEAT and Social Security certificates; paying valuations without retention.

- **Preparing the start of works (site set-up):**
  - Site cabins, hoardings, access, temporary electricity and water connections, plant.
  - Health and safety plan approved.
  - The contractor's **waste management plan** (RD 105/2008, art. 5), approved by the site supervision team and accepted by the developer.
  - Laboratory and quality control hired.
  - Setting-out record and start of works.
  - Notification of the start to the town hall, the bank, the ten-year insurer and the OCT.

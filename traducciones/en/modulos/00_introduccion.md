# CONSTRUYE
## Open-market housing development in Spain
### The complete professional course, from the plot to after-sales · Version 4.0

---

> **What this course is**
> The field manual of a residential developer in Spain. It follows the 12 real phases of a deal and, at each one, tells you which law applies, which institution to go to, what you ask for, what you get, how long it takes and which mistakes cost money.
>
> **Where the content comes from**
> The sequence of phases and tasks follows the document *Fases para el desarrollo de una promoción de viviendas* (the phases of a housing development), by courtesy of Nicolás Cabañas Yuste, extended with the legal framework, the institutions and the financial models.
>
> **How to use it**
> - Each module opens with the **Laws that apply** table and the **Where to go** table.
> - The lessons are practical bullet points: steps, documents, figures and typical mistakes.
> - Each module ends with a **checklist**, the **partners in your province** and a **quiz**.
> - **Annex A** holds the directory of the 52 provinces, with the partners for each phase.
> - **Annex B** holds the taxes of each autonomous community.
> - Modules 1, 2, 5, 7 and 8 come with their **small template**, included in the course: plot purchase, feasibility and residual method, developer loan, construction cost control, and sales and collections plan.
> - Module 1 also comes with two **templates from the professional**: the land management database and the plot's rough numbers (*números gordos*).
> - At the end, the **FINANCIALS** section presents the **optional extra**: the complete professional financial template, corrected and with up-to-date taxes, the very one the platform calculates with.

> **Legal notice for students.** Market figures are indicative (2025-2026). Regional rates and planning rules change: always confirm them in the official gazette or on the body's electronic office before you sign. This course does not replace the advice of a lawyer, a tax adviser or a registered professional.

## The 12 phases

| # | Phase | What comes out of this phase |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Plot | Plot bought (or under option) with no surprises |
| 2 | Feasibility | CRP (the developer's profit and loss account) with a GO / NO GO decision |
| 3 | Technical design | Basic and construction designs, stamped by the professional body (visado) |
| 4 | Licences | Building licence, and the site legally opened |
| 5 | Financing and contracting | Developer loan, insurance and construction contract signed |
| 6 | Construction | Building finished and taken over |
| 7 | Site control | Works on time, on cost and with documented quality |
| 8 | Sales | The pre-sales the bank requires, and the stock sold |
| 9 | Legalisation | First occupation, new building and horizontal division registered |
| 10 | Pre-handover | Homes free of defects and the notary ready |
| 11 | Handover of keys | Deeds signed, payment collected and the owners' association set up |
| 12 | After-sales and closing | Guarantees handled and the SPE closed |

## The laws you will use throughout the course

| Rule | What you use it for |
|---|---|
| **RDL 7/2015**, Texto Refundido de la Ley de Suelo y Rehabilitación Urbana (TRLSRU) | Land status, the developer's duties, negative administrative silence on building works (art. 11.4), requirements to register a new building (art. 28) |
| **Regional planning law** + the municipality's PGOU | What and how you may build, licences, deadlines. See Annex B |
| **Ley 38/1999**, de Ordenación de la Edificación (LOE) | Agents, liabilities, 1-3-10-year guarantees, ten-year insurance, payments on account (DA 1.ª) |
| **RD 314/2006**, Código Técnico de la Edificación (CTE), updated by RD 732/2019 and RD 450/2022 | The building's technical requirements |
| **RD 470/2021**, Código Estructural | Concrete and steel (replaces the EHE-08 and the EAE) |
| **RD 997/2002**, Norma de Construcción Sismorresistente (NCSE-02) | Seismic design (not in the CTE) |
| **RD 1627/1997** + **Ley 31/1995** + **Ley 32/2006** | Health and safety, risk prevention, subcontracting in construction |
| **RD 105/2008** + **Ley 7/2022** | Construction and demolition waste, contaminated land |
| **RD 842/2002** (REBT), **RD 1027/2007** (RITE), **RD 346/2011** (ICT), **RD 513/2017** (RIPCI), **RD 919/2006** (gas), **RD 88/2013** (lifts) | Installations |
| **RD 390/2021** | Energy performance certificate |
| **Ley 49/1960**, de Propiedad Horizontal (LPH) | Horizontal division, owners' association, reserve fund |
| **RDL 1/2007**, Ley General de Defensa de Consumidores (TRLGDCU) + **RD 515/1989** | The contract with the buyer, mandatory information, unfair terms, costs you may not pass on |
| **Ley 5/2019**, de Crédito Inmobiliario | The buyer's mortgage |
| **Ley 10/2010**, de Prevención del Blanqueo de Capitales | The developer is an obliged entity: identify each buyer and the origin of the funds |
| **Ley 37/1992** (IVA), **Ley 27/2014** (IS), **RDL 1/1993** (ITP-AJD), **RDL 2/2004** (local taxes: ICIO, IIVTNU, IBI) | Taxation |
| **RDL 1/2010**, Ley de Sociedades de Capital | Incorporating and closing the SPE |
| **Ley 12/2023**, por el derecho a la vivienda | Land reserves for protected housing in new schemes, information to the buyer |

**Remember:**
- **ITP and AJD are mutually exclusive** in the same purchase: if the transaction bears IVA you pay AJD; if the seller is a private individual, ITP.
- **IVA on the sale of a new home:** 10 % (4 % on special-regime protected housing). Canarias applies IGIC, and Ceuta and Melilla IPSI.
- **Navarra and País Vasco** have their own foral IS, IVA and ITP: your adviser must know those rules.
- **ICIO and IIVTNU** are municipal: check each town hall's tax by-law.
